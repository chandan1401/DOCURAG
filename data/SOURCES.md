# Evaluation & Benchmark Data Sources

The following document categories are required for comprehensive evaluation and benchmarking:

1. **Dense Text Financial & Technical Reports (PDF)**:
   - High text density corporate annual reports (10-K, 10-Q) with footnotes.
   - Whitepapers explaining systems and architectures.
2. **Tabular Documents (PDF / DOCX)**:
   - Documents with complex multi-row, multi-column tables.
   - Markdown table conversion benchmark candidates.
3. **Scanned Documents & Low-DPI Images (PDF / PNG / JPG)**:
   - Scanned receipts, historical memos, or low-contrast scans requiring OCR.
4. **Visual Elements & Charts (PDF / PNG)**:
   - Technical papers containing architectural diagrams, bar charts, and trends.
5. **Cross-Document Comparative Corpora**:
   - Multi-year comparison reports across distinct entities.
